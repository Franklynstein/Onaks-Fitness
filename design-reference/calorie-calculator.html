What to do with the number

Hit protein first

Protein keeps you full and keeps your muscle. Get it in every meal before you worry about anything else.

Track for two weeks

Use any free app. Don't change anything yet, just see what's actually going in. Most people are surprised.

Weigh in the same way

Same scale, same time, same conditions. Look at the weekly average, not the daily number.

Adjust slowly

Not moving after two weeks? Drop 100 to 150 calories. Losing too fast and feeling flat? Add some back.
